Legal & Ethical Framework

Verum Digital Intelligence & Advisory operates in full compliance with applicable laws and ethical standards. All services are conducted using lawful methodologies and responsible intelligence practices.

Scope of Operations

  • Use of publicly available and legally permissible data sources
  • Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) methodologies
  • Structured analysis and correlation of digital information
  • Advisory based on factual and evidence-based findings

Prohibited Activities

  • No unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or networks
  • No hacking, intrusion, or bypassing security mechanisms
  • No illegal surveillance or tracking
  • No violation of privacy or misuse of personal data

Confidentiality & Data Handling

All client information, case details, and findings are handled with strict confidentiality. Data is used solely for the purpose of analysis and advisory and is not disclosed without explicit client consent, except where required by law.

Advisory Disclaimer

The insights and reports provided by Verum Digital Intelligence & Advisory are based on available data and structured analysis. While all efforts are made to ensure accuracy, decisions taken based on these insights remain the responsibility of the client.

Jurisdiction

All operations are conducted in accordance with applicable laws of India. The firm operates from Pune, Maharashtra, India.
