Cyber Law (India)

Cyber law in India is primarily governed by the Information Technology Act, 2000, which addresses digital offences, data misuse, and electronic governance.

  • Section 43: Unauthorized access and data damage
  • Section 66: Computer-related offences
  • Section 66C: Identity theft
  • Section 66D: Online impersonation and fraud
  • Section 67: Objectionable content online

Understanding these provisions is essential when dealing with digital fraud, impersonation, or online disputes.

Common Cyber Fraud Patterns

UPI & Payment Fraud

Fraudsters manipulate users into approving transactions or sharing sensitive details.

Fake Job Scams

Victims are lured with offers and asked to pay fees or share personal information.

Impersonation Profiles

Fake identities are used to build trust and exploit victims financially or socially.

Phishing Attacks

Malicious links or emails designed to steal credentials and sensitive data.

Cyber Awareness Guidelines

  • Never share OTPs, passwords, or banking credentials
  • Verify identities before making financial transactions
  • Check website URLs before entering sensitive information
  • Avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links
  • Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts
  • Be cautious of urgent or high-pressure requests online
