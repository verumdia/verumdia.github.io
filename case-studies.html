UPI Fraud Pattern Analysis

CASE STUDY 01

Scenario: An individual reported multiple unauthorized UPI debit transactions shortly after receiving a call from an individual claiming to represent a banking institution.

Objective: To determine whether the incident resulted from a technical breach or a social engineering attack.

Methodology Applied:

  • Transaction sequence reconstruction
  • Interaction timeline analysis
  • Behavioral pattern evaluation
  • UPI request type assessment

Analysis & Findings: The transaction pattern indicated a sequence of "collect request" approvals initiated within a short time frame. The victim interaction suggested psychological manipulation under urgency, leading to voluntary authorization of transactions rather than system compromise.

Conclusion: The incident was identified as a social engineering-driven financial fraud, not a direct technical breach.

Advisory Outcome:

  • Awareness of collect request mechanisms
  • Verification protocols before transaction approval
  • Immediate reporting channels for financial fraud

Online Identity Verification (Relationship Context)

CASE STUDY 02

Scenario: A client sought verification of an individual with whom they had established an online relationship prior to financial involvement.

Objective: To assess the authenticity of the digital identity.

Methodology Applied:

  • Cross-platform identity correlation
  • Reverse image analysis
  • Profile behavior consistency review
  • Metadata observation

Analysis & Findings: Profile images were traced to multiple unrelated identities across platforms. Inconsistencies were identified in personal claims, timelines, and digital presence.

Conclusion: The identity was determined to be fabricated with indicators of impersonation.

Advisory Outcome:

  • Immediate disengagement recommended
  • Awareness of impersonation indicators
  • Caution in online relationship-based transactions

Pre-Business Partnership Risk Assessment

CASE STUDY 03

Scenario: A business owner intended to enter into a remote partnership and required validation of the counterpart.

Objective: To evaluate credibility and associated risk.

Methodology Applied:

  • Digital footprint mapping
  • Professional presence validation
  • Domain and communication linkage checks
  • Historical activity assessment

Analysis & Findings: The subject exhibited minimal verifiable professional history, with inconsistencies in claimed experience and affiliations.

Conclusion: The risk profile was assessed as elevated due to lack of verifiable credibility.

Advisory Outcome:

  • Recommended structured due diligence
  • Suggested phased engagement model
  • Risk mitigation before financial commitment

Fake Job Offer Scam Investigation

CASE STUDY 04

Scenario: An applicant received a job offer requiring payment of onboarding and processing fees.

Objective: To determine legitimacy of the hiring entity.

Methodology Applied:

  • Domain authenticity verification
  • Communication pattern analysis
  • Corporate presence validation
  • Offer structure evaluation

Analysis & Findings: The entity lacked legitimate registration indicators. The domain was recently created and designed to mimic a legitimate organization.

Conclusion: The case was identified as a fraudulent recruitment scam.

Advisory Outcome:

  • Advised non-engagement
  • Reporting recommendations
  • Awareness regarding recruitment fraud patterns

Phishing Link Threat Assessment

CASE STUDY 06

Scenario: A user received a link claiming to require urgent credential verification for a financial account.

Objective: To assess whether the link posed a security threat.

Methodology Applied:

  • URL structure analysis
  • Domain registration review
  • Interface comparison with legitimate services

Analysis & Findings: The domain closely resembled an official source but contained structural anomalies and was recently registered.

Conclusion: The link was identified as a phishing attempt designed for credential harvesting.

Advisory Outcome:

  • Immediate avoidance
  • Credential security recommendations
  • Phishing awareness measures
